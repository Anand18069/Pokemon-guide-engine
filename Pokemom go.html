🌍 What is Pokémon GO?

A Pokémon adventure in the real world.

Pokémon GO is a location-based mobile game where you explore the real world while discovering and catching Pokémon. You can collect Pokémon, evolve them, complete research, visit PokéStops, battle in Gyms, participate in raids, and compete against other Trainers.

🎮 How to Play

These are the basic things every new Trainer should know.

1️⃣ Explore

Walk around your local area and look at the in-game map. Pokémon and other locations can appear as you explore.

2️⃣ Catch Pokémon

When you encounter a Pokémon, tap it to enter the catching screen. Throw a Poké Ball toward the Pokémon to try to catch it.

3️⃣ Visit PokéStops

PokéStops can provide useful items such as Poké Balls and other supplies. Spin them when you're nearby.

4️⃣ Power Up & Evolve

Pokémon can become stronger using resources such as Stardust and Candy. Many Pokémon can also evolve when you have enough Candy.

5️⃣ Battle

As you progress, you can participate in Gym battles, Raid Battles, and Trainer Battles.

🎒 Important Resources

You'll see these everywhere in your Pokémon GO journey.

⭐ XP Experience Points help increase your Trainer level.
✨ Stardust Used for powering up Pokémon and other features.
🍬 Candy Pokémon-specific resources used for evolution and powering up.
🔴 Poké Balls Used to catch Pokémon.
🥚 Eggs Eggs can hatch after you walk the required distance.
🪙 PokéCoins The game's premium currency.

💡 Beginner Tips

Small things that can make your journey much easier.

1
Don't spend Stardust randomly.

Stardust becomes increasingly valuable as you progress, so think before investing heavily in a Pokémon.

2
Keep catching Pokémon.

Even Pokémon you don't need immediately can provide XP and Candy.

3
Learn about Pokémon types.

Understanding type matchups is extremely useful when battling.

4
Don't transfer everything immediately.

Some Pokémon may become useful later for evolution, research, trading, or collection purposes.

5
Play safely.

Pay attention to your surroundings while playing. Never stare at your phone while crossing roads.
